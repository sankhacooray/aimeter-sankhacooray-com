Claude Meter · free for macOS

Your Claude usage,
next to your battery.

A tiny menu bar app that shows how much of your Claude plan you've used, so you don't have to open Settings → Usage.

  • Session, weekly and per-model limits, with reset countdowns
  • Uses your own Claude Code login; only talks to Anthropic
  • Free and open source, about 450 lines of Swift
Download for Mac v1.0 · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon & Intel
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bsc2fast/claude-meter-macos/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

Or paste this in Terminal. Needs Claude Code signed in to a Pro or Max plan. If macOS blocks the download, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
